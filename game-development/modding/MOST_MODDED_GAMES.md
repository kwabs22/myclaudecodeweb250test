## Introduction

Game modding has transformed from a niche hobby in the 1980s to a multi-billion dollar industry influencer. Some games have transcended their original vision through modding, spawning entirely new genres, games, and even careers.

This document examines the most modded games in history, analyzing what made them modding phenomena and how they shaped the gaming industry.

### Defining "Most Modded"

We measure "most modded" by:
- **Total mod count**: Number of mods created
- **Active modders**: Size of modding community
- **Cultural impact**: Influence on gaming and modding culture
- **Longevity**: How long modding has remained active
- **Quality**: Notable mods and total conversions
- **Industry influence**: Games/genres spawned from mods

## The Top 20 Most Modded Games

### 1. **The Elder Scrolls V: Skyrim** (2011)

**Platform**: PC, Xbox, PlayStation
**Engine**: Creation Engine
**Total Mods**: ~70,000+ (Nexus Mods) + 28,000+ (Steam Workshop)
**Active Modders**: 10,000+

**Why It's #1**:
- Official modding tools (Creation Kit)
- Bethesda actively encourages modding
- Massive open world perfect for expansion
- Approachable scripting (Papyrus)
- Multiple re-releases keeping it fresh (SE, AE, VR)
- Console mod support (Xbox, PlayStation)

**Modding Highlights**:
- **SkyUI**: Complete UI overhaul
- **Enderal**: Full total conversion (new game)
- **Requiem**: Complete gameplay overhaul
- **Beyond Skyrim**: Recreating all of Tamriel
- **SKSE64**: Script extender enabling advanced mods

**Cultural Impact**:
- Defined modern modding culture
- "Skyrim modding" is practically its own hobby
- Kept game relevant for 13+ years
- Spawned careers in game development

**Fun Fact**: There are mods that turn Skyrim into Dark Souls, Minecraft, a survival game, a flight simulator, and more.

**Tools**: Creation Kit, SKSE64, xEdit, Mod Organizer 2

### 2. **Minecraft** (2011)

**Platform**: Java (PC), Bedrock (Multi-platform)
**Engine**: Custom (Java)
**Total Mods**: ~62,000+ (CurseForge) + countless custom
**Active Modders**: 50,000+

**Why It's #2**:
- Java-based (easy to decompile and mod)
- Mojang/Microsoft encourages modding
- Infinite creative possibilities
- Educational use drives modding
- Younger audience learns programming through it
- Multiple mod loaders (Forge, Fabric, Bukkit)

**Modding Highlights**:
- **Forge**: Most popular mod loader
- **Fabric**: Modern, lightweight alternative
- **Pixelmon**: Full Pokémon game in Minecraft
- **Thermal Expansion**: Industry-changing tech mod
- **Create**: Mechanical engineering in Minecraft

**Cultural Impact**:
- Introduced millions to programming
- Educational tool worldwide
- Modpacks as popular as vanilla
- Spawned careers for thousands

**Notable Stats**:
- Some modpacks have 200+ mods
- Feed The Beast revolutionized modpack distribution
- Mods exist for every interest imaginable

**Tools**: Minecraft Forge, Fabric, MCreator, IntelliJ IDEA

## What Makes a Game Moddable?

### Technical Factors

**1. Accessible File Formats**
- ✅ XML, JSON, plain text
- ✅ Uncompressed assets
- ❌ Proprietary, encrypted formats

**2. Official Tools**
- ✅ Level editors (Hammer, Creation Kit)
- ✅ SDKs and documentation
- ✅ Example content

**3. Moddable Architecture**
- ✅ Plugin systems
- ✅ Script support
- ✅ Virtual file systems

**4. Engine Accessibility**
- ✅ .NET (easy decompile)
- ✅ Java (Minecraft)
- ✅ Lua scripting
- ⚠️ C++ (harder but possible)

---

### Developer Support

**1. Official Stance**
- ✅ Bethesda: Actively encourages
- ✅ Mojang: Supports modding
- ⚠️ Rockstar: Tolerates (inconsistently)
- ❌ Nintendo: Generally opposes

**2. Tools Provided**
- Best: Full editor (Creation Kit, Hammer)
- Good: Documentation and SDK
- Okay: Doesn't block modding
- Poor: Actively prevents modding

**3. Workshop Integration**
- Steam Workshop (easiest)
- Nexus Mods integration
- In-game mod browsers

---

### Community Factors

**1. Game Longevity**
- Long-lived games develop larger communities
- Regular updates keep modders engaged

**2. Gameplay**
- Sandbox games ideal (Minecraft, Cities)
- Open worlds (Skyrim, GTA)
- Customization-focused (Sims)

**3. Accessibility**
- Easy to learn, hard to master
- Clear systems to build on

---

### Cultural Factors

**1. Timing**
- Released when PC modding was strong
- Pre-DLC era (mods filled gaps)

**2. Pricing**
- Affordable base game
- Regular sales

**3. PC Focus**
- PC-first releases easier to mod
- Console versions may limit mods
