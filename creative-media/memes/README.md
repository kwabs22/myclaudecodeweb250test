# Memes

Reference text on internet memes, slang and viral content. There are no runnable pages.

| Document | What it covers |
|---|---|
| [Meme dictionary](MEME_DICTIONARY.md) | Meme repositories on GitHub, categories and formats, developer memes, a glossary and meme APIs |
| [Country memes, part 1](COUNTRY_SPECIFIC_MEMES.md) | England, Germany, Japan, South Korea and Nigeria |
| [Country memes, part 2](COUNTRY_MEMES_PART2.md) | South Africa, Brazil, Argentina, China, Russia, Mongolia and Thailand |

Memes and slang date quickly. These documents were compiled in November 2025 from Know Your Meme, news reports and platform discussion, so treat them as a snapshot. Some entries quote strong language or national stereotypes in order to explain them.
