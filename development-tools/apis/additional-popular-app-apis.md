## Why These Were Missing

1. **Social Media Dominance** - Most social platforms are extremely popular but weren't in the original list
2. **Productivity Tools** - Notion, Trello, Asana are widely used by developers and businesses
3. **E-commerce Giants** - Shopify, eBay, Etsy, Amazon are massive but weren't included
4. **Communication Tools** - Zoom, Teams, Discord became essential during/after pandemic
5. **Payment Processors** - PayPal and Square are as popular as Stripe
6. **Gaming Platforms** - Twitch, Steam have huge developer communities
7. **Business Tools** - Salesforce, HubSpot, Zendesk are standard in enterprise
