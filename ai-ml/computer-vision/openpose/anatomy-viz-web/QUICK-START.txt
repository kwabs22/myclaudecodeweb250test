🦴 ANATOMY VISUALIZATION - QUICK START 🦴

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

📱 ON YOUR PHONE (Android/iPhone):

1. Transfer "index.html" to your phone
   - Email it to yourself
   - Use Google Drive/Dropbox
   - AirDrop (iPhone)

2. Open with browser:
   - Chrome (recommended)
   - Firefox
   - Safari

3. Allow camera when asked

4. Stand back so your whole body is visible

5. Click buttons to explore!

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

💻 ON YOUR COMPUTER:

METHOD 1 (Easiest):
   Just double-click "index.html"

METHOD 2 (Better):
   cd anatomy-viz-web
   python -m http.server 8000
   Then open: http://localhost:8000

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

🎮 CONTROLS:

🦴 Skeleton  - Toggle bones on/off
💪 Muscles   - Toggle muscles (watch them activate!)
🏷️ Labels   - Show anatomical names
📚 Info      - Educational information

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

🎯 TRY THIS:

1. Click "🦴 Skeleton" - see your bones!
2. Click "💪 Muscles" - add muscles
3. Bend your elbow - watch biceps turn RED!
4. Extend your leg - watch quadriceps activate!
5. Click "🏷️ Labels" - learn bone names
6. Click "📚 Info" - read educational content

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

⚠️ TROUBLESHOOTING:

Camera not working?
   → Click lock icon in address bar
   → Allow camera permission
   → Refresh page

Slow/laggy?
   → Close other tabs
   → Use Chrome browser
   → Move closer to camera

Not detecting body?
   → Stand further back
   → Make sure full body visible
   → Improve lighting
