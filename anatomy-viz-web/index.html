Welcome to Anatomy Visualization!

This educational tool shows your skeletal and muscular systems in real-time using AI pose detection.

How to Use:

1. Allow camera access when prompted
2. Stand in front of camera
3. Toggle layers to explore anatomy
4. Move around to see muscle activation!

Features:

🦴 Skeleton: Major bones overlaid on your body
💪 Muscles: Watch them activate as you move
🏷️ Labels: Learn anatomical names
